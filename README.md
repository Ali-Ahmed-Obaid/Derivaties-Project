# The Derivatives Time Machine

### Could a Gulf airline’s 2023 hedge have protected it from the 2026 oil shock?

A no-hindsight derivatives research project examining whether a Gulf airline could reduce fuel-price risk using **Brent futures**, **Black–76 options**, and a **jet-fuel basis-risk analysis**.

The study designs a hypothetical hedge using only information available in 2023, then evaluates its performance during a simulated 2026 oil shock.

**Result: 🟡 PARTIALLY CORRECT**

The Brent hedge reduced exposure to rising energy prices but did not fully offset the airline’s higher jet-fuel costs because of **basis risk** and differences between Brent crude and jet-fuel price movements.

### Key Concepts

`Derivatives` • `Brent Futures` • `Black–76` • `Options Pricing` • `Hedging` • `Basis Risk` • `Energy Markets`
